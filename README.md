# RECLAIM – Campus Recovery System

> **Lost it. Found it. Reclaim it.**

RECLAIM is a modern campus Lost & Found platform designed to make recovering lost belongings faster, safer, and more organized.

Instead of depending on notice boards, WhatsApp groups, or a dedicated Lost & Found team, RECLAIM provides a centralized platform where students and campus users can report lost or found items, discover possible matches, verify ownership, and complete a structured recovery process.

## 🎯 Problem

Students frequently lose personal belongings such as:

- ID cards
- Wallets
- Backpacks
- Earphones
- Keys
- Books
- Water bottles
- Electronic devices

Traditional campus Lost & Found systems are often fragmented across different departments, security desks, notice boards, and messaging groups.

This creates three major problems:

1. Lost items are difficult to discover.
2. Found items are difficult to connect with their owners.
3. There is no reliable ownership verification process.
